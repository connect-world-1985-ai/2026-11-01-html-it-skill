For the review

Picking two directions

  1. The A variants keep each screen self-contained, with large single actions. The B variants spread actions across lists and sheets and keep people in context.
  2. You can mix directions per screen. Some pairs work better together, such as 3B with 6B, where the error sheet sits over the grouped picker.
  3. Questions to settle in the room: Is map-first search worth the extra build cost? Can a saved insurance card skip the photo step?
