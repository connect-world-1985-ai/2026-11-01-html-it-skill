Current hypothesis

The SameSite=None session cookie has no Partitioned attribute, so Safari drops it after the redirect back from the identity provider.

Ruled out

  • ruled outServer clock skew. NTP checked on all nodes.
  • ruled outToken lifetime. Unchanged since 8.3.
  • ruled outLoad balancer stickiness. Same result with a single node.
