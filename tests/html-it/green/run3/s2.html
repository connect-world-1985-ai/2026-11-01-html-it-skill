    Booking app: six screen sketches

    Six mobile screen sketches for the appointment booking app cover the whole booking flow, from the home screen to a slot that was taken in the meantime. The team compares the layouts and picks two directions to take forward. Mark your two picks on the board below, then paste the message under it into the team channel.

    Prepared 2026-11-03 for the booking app team. The sketches are low-fidelity wireframes: grey bars stand for text, and dates, times, names and labels are placeholders.

    Decision needed: two directions out of these layouts. Who decides and by when is not given in the source. What "direction" means here, two of the six screens or two overall layout styles, is To be confirmed.

    The flow at a glance

    The six screens are steps of one booking. A patient starts on Home, searches for a practice, picks a slot and confirms. The booking then either succeeds, or fails because the slot is gone; the error screen leads straight back to choosing a time.

    Sketch board

    Filter by flow stage with the chips. To compare two sketches side by side, press Mark as pick on two of them, then Compare picks. Each sketch is a separate small page inside a frame, so its styles do not mix with this page.

    Glossary

    Chips
    Small rounded buttons that each switch one filter value on or off; here, one per medical specialty.
    Filter sheet
    A panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen and holds filter controls, then closes to show the results.
    Week strip
    A row of seven day buttons for one week; tapping a day shows its free times.
