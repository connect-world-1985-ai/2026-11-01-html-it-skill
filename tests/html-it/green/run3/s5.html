Backend onboarding

    Your first week as a backend engineer

    By the end of your first week you have a working laptop and accounts, a local build of the core project with its integration tests passing, and your first pull request shipped and demoed at the Friday sync. Work through the eleven tasks below day by day, tick each one off, and send your buddy the progress message at the bottom whenever you finish a day.

    For backend engineers in their first week, and for the buddy who helps them. The plan counts in working days from your first day, not in calendar dates.

    The week at a glance

    The week moves from setting up, to building, to understanding the code base, to shipping. Each day builds on the one before: you cannot run the integration tests without the accounts from Day 1, and your first pull request needs the local build from Day 2.

    What this page cannot do

    The page is a single file that works offline. It cannot share state between people, so it cannot show your buddy your progress, and it does not update Jira or any other system when you tick a task. Ticks live in this browser only. The progress message is the way to share them; a printout or a screenshot works too.

    The checklist does not say where ONB-01 "How we work" and ADR-007 event sourcing decision are stored, how to request the accounts, or which command builds the core project. Your buddy can point you to all of these on Day 1.

    Glossary

    Architecture decision record (ADR)
    A short document that records one architecture decision, its reasons and its consequences.
    Buddy
    The colleague assigned to help you through your first weeks and to follow your progress.
    Docker
    A tool that runs software in isolated containers; the integration tests use it to start the services they depend on.
    Event sourcing
    A way of storing data as the full sequence of changes (events) that happened, instead of only the current state.
    Friday sync
    The team's regular meeting on Fridays, where new hires demo their first pull request. Its time and format are not given in the checklist.
    Good first issue
    An issue the team has marked as small and well understood, suitable as a newcomer's first change.
    Integration tests
    Tests that check several parts of the system working together, rather than one class or function on its own.
    Jira, Bitbucket, Teams
    The team's issue tracker, code and pull-request host, and chat and meeting tool.
    VPN
    Virtual private network: the encrypted connection that lets your laptop reach the company's internal systems.
