    Patient portal UI audit

    The UI audit of the patient portal for release R26.11 patient portal release found one issue on each of the first ten screens, and all ten are still open. Developers: open a screen page below, fix its issue and compare with the screenshot. Designers: fill in screens 11 to 40 and put the current PNGs into the assets/ folder.

    Audit by the design team: 40 full-page screenshots, 10 described so far. Screenshots: PNG files not provided.

    Why this is a folder, not one file. The 40 screenshots are about 52 MB. Embedded in one page they would grow to about 70 MB, far above the usual 20 to 25 MB limit for email and Teams attachments, and the copies would go out of date each time the designers edit a PNG. This overview and the ten screen pages are plain HTML files that open offline and show each PNG straight from assets/. Share the whole folder as a zip: unzip it and open index.html.

    What the ten issues have in common

    Four issues make a screen hard to understand: the unlabelled password field (01), toggles that do not say what on and off mean (09), unread messages marked only by colour (05), and a search without an empty state (10). Three are about consistency and layout: three competing primary buttons (02), mixed date formats (03), and messages that overflow on a 390 px wide phone (06). Two are about safe actions and complete information: cancelling an appointment without confirmation (04) and downloads without a file size (07). One concerns personal data: the profile screen shows the insurance number in full instead of masking it (08). This grouping was made for this overview; the audit does not rank the issues by severity.

    Glossary

    Empty state
    What a screen shows when there is nothing to list, for example a search with no results.
    Masking
    Showing only part of a sensitive value, such as the last digits of a number, and hiding the rest.
    Primary button
    The single most important action on a screen, drawn with the strongest visual weight.
