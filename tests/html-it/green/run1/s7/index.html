    Patient portal UI audit

    The UI audit of R26.11 the audited portal release of the patient portal has written up one issue on each of the first 10 of 40 screens. Two of them should be fixed first because they can harm patients directly: the profile shows the full insurance number, and appointments can be cancelled with a single tap. Issues for screens 11 to 40 are still to be filled in by the designers.

    Prepared 3 November 2026 for the portal designers and developers. Each screen has its own page; open the one you are working on from the table below.

    What to fix first

    Most of the ten issues make the portal harder to use. Two go further, and are the suggested starting point:

    • Screen 08, Profile: the insurance number is shown in full instead of being masked. Anyone who sees the screen, or a screenshot of it, can read the number.
    • Screen 04, Appointment detail: cancelling an appointment needs no confirmation step, so one mis-tap loses a patient’s appointment.

    Three further issues are accessibility problems (screens 01, 05 and 09). They exclude screen-reader users and people with colour-vision deficiencies and are usually quick to fix. This order is a suggestion of this write-up; the audit notes themselves do not rank the issues.

    How this folder works

    This audit is a folder rather than a single HTML file, for two reasons. The 40 full-page screenshots are 1.0 to 1.6 MB each, about 52 MB together; embedded in one page they would grow to about 70 MB, far past the 20 to 25 MB that email and most chat attachments accept. And the designers keep editing the original PNGs, so copies embedded in a page would go out of date.

    PathWhat it holds
    index.htmlThis overview: the summary, the searchable table of screens, and how to use the folder.
    parts/01-login.html to parts/10-help.htmlOne page per screen with the issue, a suggested direction, the screenshot, and links to the previous and next screen.
    assets/The original screenshot PNGs, under the file names in the table above. The designers replace them here with each new export.

    Screenshots not provided yet. The PNG files were not part of the material this audit was built from, so assets/ is empty and every screen page shows a labelled placeholder. As soon as a designer copies, for example, 01-login.png into assets/, the login page shows it. No image in this folder is invented.

    To share the audit, zip the whole folder and send the zip. Readers unzip it and open index.html; everything works offline, and the screen pages link back to this overview.

    Glossary

    Masking
    Showing only part of a sensitive value, for example the last four characters of an insurance number, with the rest replaced by dots.
    Confirmation step
    A dialog or second step that asks the user to confirm an action that cannot easily be undone, such as cancelling an appointment.
