    Glossary

    Retrospective (retro)
    A short team meeting at the end of a sprint to look at what went well, what to improve, and what to change next.
    Flaky test
    A test that sometimes passes and sometimes fails on the same code, so its result says little about the change.
    Quarantine (of a test)
    Moving a flaky test out of the run that blocks merges, while it still runs and is reported, until it is fixed.
    Runbook
    A written, step-by-step procedure for a recurring operational task, such as rolling back a release.
