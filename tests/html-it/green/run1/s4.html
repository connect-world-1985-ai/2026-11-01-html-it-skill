    Builds on the platform team got clearly faster and steadier in week 45 (2 to 6 November 2026), but milestone M3 WildFly 36 migration is at risk: it stands at 68 %, seven points short of the 75 % due by 6 November 2026.     What needs attention

    Milestone M3 is behind plan. The WildFly 36 migration is 68 % done against a target of 75 % by Friday 6 November 2026. With the target date at the end of this week, the status note rates the milestone at risk.

    The bug KT-902 blocked on a vendor patch is a priority-1 (P1) bug that waits for a patch from an outside vendor. The status note does not say which vendor, when the patch is expected, or whether the bug affects M3. The other two open P1 bugs, KT-881 open P1 bug, title not provided and KT-915 open P1 bug, title not provided, are not reported as blocked.

    The status note names no owners or next steps. A useful next step is for the owner of M3 to decide this week whether to add effort or move the 75 % target date, and to add owners to next week's status.

    Build time

    Half of all builds now finish within 11.2 minutes (the p50), 3.6 minutes faster than in week 44. The slow end improved even more: 95 % of builds finish within 23.0 minutes (the p95), 8.5 minutes faster than the 31.5 minutes of week 44. The status note does not say what caused the improvement.

    Flaky tests

    The number of flaky tests fell from 37 to 22, a drop of 15 tests or 41 %. Fewer flaky tests means fewer reruns and fewer red builds that are not real failures.

    Milestone M3: WildFly 36 migration

    M3 WildFly 36 migration is 68 % complete. The plan was 75 % by 6 November 2026, the last working day of week 45, so the milestone is marked at risk. The status note does not say how progress is measured or when the milestone is due in full.

    Glossary

    p50
    Median build time: half of the builds were faster than this, half slower.
    p95
    95th percentile build time: 95 of every 100 builds were faster than this. It shows how slow the slow builds are.
    Flaky test
    A test that sometimes passes and sometimes fails on the same code, so its result cannot be trusted.
    P1, P2
    Bug priorities in the team's tracker. P1 is the highest; P2 is the next level down.
    WildFly
    Red Hat's open-source Java application server. Milestone M3 is the migration to version 36.
