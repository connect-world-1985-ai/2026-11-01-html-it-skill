    How the six screens connect

    The screens follow one booking: the patient starts at Home, searches for a specialty, picks a time, and confirms. Confirming ends either in Success or, if the time was booked in the meantime, in the Error screen, which offers three other times and leads back to Confirm.

    Glossary

    Chip
    A small rounded button that toggles one option, such as a specialty.
    Filter sheet
    A panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen over the content; here it holds the search filters (distance, earliest date, language).
    Week strip
    A horizontal row of the days of one week; tapping a day shows its times, and arrows move to the previous or next week.
