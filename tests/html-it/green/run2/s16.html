Patient portal, password reset prototype

    Password reset prototype

    This is the clickable prototype of the new password reset flow in the patient portal, built for the usability test on Thursday 2026-11-06. Testers go from the "Forgot password?" link on the login screen to the success screen in five steps; the moderator uses the controls below the prototype to reset to the start or jump to any screen, including the expired-link error. No email is sent and no account is checked: every screen is simulated in this file.

    Prepared 3 November 2026 for the patient portal team and the test moderator. To be confirmed The test date is given as Thursday 2026-11-06, but 6 November 2026 is a Friday; check which day is meant.

    What to try

    Open this file in a browser on the test laptop. It works offline. Give the tester the task below and let them click through without help. Before each new tester, press Reset to start; to hide this text and show only the prototype, press Tester view.

    1. "You have forgotten your password for the patient portal. Set a new one."
    2. Optional second task, started by the moderator with Jump to: Link expired: "You open the reset email after a long lunch break. Get a working link."

    What is not real. No email leaves this page and no account exists behind it. Any email address leads to the "Check your inbox" screen, exactly as the real portal must behave. Opening the reset link is simulated with yellow, dashed "Prototype only" buttons on that screen. The portal cannot know earlier passwords here, so the rule "not one of the last 5" is met unless the moderator switches it off in the controls.

    How the flow works

    The happy path has five screens. The login screen's "Forgot password?" link opens the email form; sending it always leads to "Check your inbox", where the Resend button stays disabled for 60 seconds so that people do not trigger a burst of emails. The link in the email opens the new-password screen, whose checklist turns each rule green as the person types. Saving leads to the success screen and back to sign in.

    Two paths leave the happy path. An email address with no account shows the same "Check your inbox" screen, word for word, because of SEC-31 never reveal whether an account exists: a different message would let anyone test which addresses have a portal account, which is called account enumeration. A link opened more than 30 minutes after it was sent shows "This link has expired" with a button that sends a new link and returns to step 3.

    Glossary

    Account enumeration
    Finding out which accounts exist by watching how a system answers, for example a different message for known and unknown email addresses.
