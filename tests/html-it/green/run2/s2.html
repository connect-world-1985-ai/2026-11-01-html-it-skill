Team note: booking app sketches

    Booking app screen sketches

    Six wireframe sketches cover the whole booking flow of the mobile appointment booking app, from the home screen to a successful or failed booking. The team compares their layouts and picks two directions to develop further: use the Pick buttons on the board below, compare your two picks side by side, then post them in the team channel.

    Prepared 3 November 2026 for the booking app team, from the team's six screen ideas. Doctor names, practices, dates and times inside the sketches are placeholders, not real data.

    Decision needed: which two sketches become the design directions. The notes do not say whether a "direction" means two of these six screens or two layout styles to apply across the flow, nor who decides and by when; both are To be confirmed.

    How the screens connect

    The six screens form one flow. A patient starts on Home, searches for a doctor, picks a free slot, checks the details and confirms. Normally the booking succeeds; if someone else booked the same slot in the meantime, the Error screen offers the next three free slots and the patient confirms one of those instead. The notes do not say exactly when the Error screen appears; the diagram assumes it appears after pressing confirm.

    Glossary

    Direction
    A layout approach the design team develops further after this comparison. Whether it means a screen or a style across screens is to be confirmed.
    Filter sheet
    A panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen and holds the search filters.
    Specialty chips
    Small rounded buttons in a row, each selecting one medical specialty.
    Time grid
    The times of the chosen day laid out as a grid of buttons.
    Week strip
    A row of seven day buttons at the top of the slot picker; tapping one shows that day's times.
    Wireframe
    A deliberately plain sketch of a screen that shows layout and content, not final colours or type.
