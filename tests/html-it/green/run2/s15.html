    The ideas in plain words:

    • Idea 1 takes a test that fails at random out of the main run automatically, so it stops blocking merges while someone fixes it. See quarantine in the glossary.
    • Idea 2 names a reviewer on duty in turn, who aims to give every pull request a first review within 24 hours of it being opened.
    • Idea 3 keeps Wednesday afternoons free of meetings, so the team has one block of focus time in the middle of the week.
    • Idea 4 moves the retro from every two weeks to every three weeks, which means fewer retros with more to discuss in each.

    Glossary

    Retro (retrospective)
    A meeting at the end of a sprint where the team looks back at what worked and what did not, and agrees on changes to try.
    Flaky test
    A test that sometimes passes and sometimes fails on the same code, so its result cannot be trusted.
    Quarantine (of a test)
    Taking an unreliable test out of the blocking test run, while still running and tracking it, until it is fixed.
    Median review wait
    The middle value of the time pull requests waited for review: half waited less, half waited longer.
    Runbook
    A written, step-by-step procedure for an operational task, used by whoever is on call.
